Streamer Toolkit

Free tools for any streamer. Everything runs in your browser: your images are never uploaded anywhere.

Drag any text on a panel to move it. Click a text (or its button under the panel) to change the words, size, color and font. Put any image or GIF behind the text. Add as many panels as you need. Tip: click a panel, then use the arrow keys to move the selected text (Shift = faster).

Emote & Badge Resizer

Upload one picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or an animated GIF) and get every size Twitch asks for, ready to upload. Keeps transparency and GIF animation.

Background Remover

Makes a plain-color background transparent. Works with PNG, JPG, WebP and animated GIFs.

This tool removes solid-color backgrounds (green screen, white, black, a flat color). It is not an AI cut-out, so busy photo backgrounds won't come out clean. For those, remove most of it here, then clean up the rest with the Erase brush.
